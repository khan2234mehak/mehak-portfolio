What I build

About me

Data scientist and machine learning engineer in the making, with the full-stack skills to put models into products.

I work across the full ML lifecycle - data preprocessing, EDA, feature engineering, predictive modelling and deployment - and build end-to-end pipelines and REST APIs with Python, Scikit-learn and Flask. I have hands-on experience with time series forecasting, classification, regression and database-backed systems (SQL / NoSQL).

On the web side I build with React, Node.js and Express, which is why my projects ship with a real interface, authentication and reports rather than living in a notebook.
